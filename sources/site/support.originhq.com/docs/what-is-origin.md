# Source: https://support.originhq.com/docs/what-is-origin

Origin is an endpoint AI observability platform that inventories, observes, and measures the usage of AI agents at scale, giving stakeholders the necessary operational intelligence into the state of AI in their organization. This observability is the foundation for everything from making defensible investments in AI to maximizing its adoption and efficiency without compromising the safety of your organization.

## 

Key Capabilities

- **Investigations** — Trace any incident to its origin prompt.
- **Audit and Compliance** — A complete record of every prompt and every agent decision.
- **Inventory/Visibility** — Complete inventory of AI and computer use agents.
- **Behavioral Baselines** — Detect anomalies by understanding normal.
