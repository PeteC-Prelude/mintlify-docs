description: "The Origin console has 4 primary views designed to provide a simple gateway to the data stored within the solution:"
---

- **Analytics Graph** - the graph provides a series of out of box dashboards and queries designed to provide insight into the AI usage within an organization.  The Analytics graph also provides a free form chat interface allowing users to query anything in the system.
- **Explorer** - A visual exploration of data within the Origin solution.  Allowing users to explore high level activities and drill down by agents, identities, endpoints and other attributes.
- **Libraries** - A management interface to create/save/edit kernels (saved queries) and canvases (dashboard pages)
- **Settings** - A management interface allowing Tenant Admins to setup and configure the Origin solution.
